## Remaining external inputs and explicit limitations

For literal requirement-by-requirement comparison, see `blueprint_comparison.md`; this batch is detailed in `steps_1_to_7_status.md`. Actual reviewed regional/dose content remains absent, so live context/dose checks stay unavailable. Free-text/multiple-medicine schedule interpretation, clinical approval and deferred deployment/language/offline requirements remain outside those checks. The implemented rows describe the current prototype, not completion of every blueprint requirement.

- Physician/pharmacist sign-off remains pending. Dose tables contain no approved entries; the app reports that the check is unavailable and requires doctor review.
- Wording, speech, microphone and touch require target-device/native-speaker checks. Prepared danger recordings work without a browser voice; other browser playback requires a matching voice. Regional accuracy/TTS/full interface translations remain unvalidated or absent. Twenty-seven wider question-catalogue Urdu prompts remain unprepared; all 17 unique Urdu danger prompts are prepared.
- No clinical reviewers or real-service credentials are currently available, as confirmed by the user. Clinician identities and facility/stock data remain synthetic. The delivery adapter is prepared but inactive; no actual notifications or facility receipt were established. Production accounts and real-patient deployment are not implemented.
- Offline operation is a manual capture fallback. Full automatic sync/PWA and validated provincial/DHIS2 integration remain deferred.
- The verifiers cover deterministic facts and constrained catalogue structure, not unrestricted clinical prose or clinical truth. Catalogue/rules/diagrams/audio remain unreviewed. Retention requires an explicit institution-selected policy.

See `verification.md` for executed checks, `consultation_architecture.md` for storage/workflow details, and `clinical_content_register.md` for approval status. Passing authored software tests does not establish patient outcomes or clinical accuracy.
